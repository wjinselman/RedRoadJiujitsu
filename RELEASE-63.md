- The gym QR now opens `checkin.html`, a focused attendance page.
- The normal member dashboard has no check-in button or attendance prompt.
- The staff attendance header has no extra phone-check-in launch button.
- Members scan, sign in when needed and confirm the inferred class.
- Kiosk mode remains an optional staff setting and QR phone check-in remains the default.
