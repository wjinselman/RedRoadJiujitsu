A nearby BJJ option for Healdton-area students.

Students coming from Healdton can train Brazilian Jiu Jitsu at Red Road in Lone Grove with kids and adult classes, straightforward monthly pricing, and No-Gi training on Tuesday and Thursday nights.

Red Road Jiu Jitsu is located at 41 Almond Dr, Lone Grove, Oklahoma 73401. Kids class runs 6:00–7:00 PM and adult class runs 7:00–8:00 PM. No-Gi training is offered Tuesday and Thursday nights.

All levels are welcome. If you are new to Brazilian Jiu Jitsu, you do not need previous grappling or martial arts experience to start.
